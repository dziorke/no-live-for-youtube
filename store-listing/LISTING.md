## Name

No Live for YouTube

## Short summary

Hide live, upcoming, and archived YouTube streams and keep them out of autoplay.

## Testing notice

This extension is still being tested and may not always work. YouTube changes
its page structure frequently, which can temporarily affect detection.

## Full description

No Live for YouTube keeps livestream content out of YouTube feeds,
recommendations, search results, and autoplay.

Choose whether to hide currently live broadcasts, upcoming streams, and
automatically archived livestream recordings. When autoplay selects a blocked
stream, the extension advances to the next video. Deliberately opening a live
video remains possible.

Everything runs locally in the browser. There are no analytics, ads, accounts,
remote services, or developer-operated servers.

Features:

- hides currently live broadcasts;
- hides scheduled and upcoming streams;
- hides archived livestream recordings;
- skips blocked streams selected by autoplay;
- provides independent settings for each category;
- stores preferences locally.

YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.
